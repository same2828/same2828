# TLDR

- `"ose"` suffix = OK in moderation (dextrose, glucose) even if they are derived from wheat
- `"in"` suffix = AVOID (dextrin)
  - In Australia wheat based dextrins must be labelled as `"dextrin (wheat)"`
    - Can eat dextrins if they don't declare wheat
