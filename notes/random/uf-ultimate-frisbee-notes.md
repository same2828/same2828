# Backhand

Power Grip = All fingers on the inside

Frisbee into palm and fingers wrap around disc (like shaking hand)

- Kurt Gibson = Put all fingers on inside at deepest groove
- Rowan McDonnell = All fingers on the rim (not exactly at deepest groove)
- Brodie Smith = Do NOT have all fingers tightened on edge or all spread out in middle
  - Index finger on rim
  - Middle finger pushing on middle of disk
  - Ring & Pinky finger tight against rim
  - Only to throw far/huck do we move all fingers closer in (and tighten up)
- Frisbee Rob
  - Does NOT use/like the power grip (all fingers on inside)
  - Index finger on the rim for push pass/short pass/floater
- Ryan Lowe
  - First knuckle of index finger on edge of disk
  - Change angle of disc so that it is completely inside your palm (no gaps)

Note: Use your left hand to balance frisbee when pulling it back
Note: Can also use fan grip for short pass (to prevent airbounce)

# Pulling

- Top bend of index finger on disc edge (to guide)
- Cock back/bend wrist WHEN PULLING BACK
- Don't cock back/bend wrist into elbow but rather just the chest
- Step 45degree forwards (try not to step side by side to left leg)
- When reaching backwards, don't reach behind shoulder (so that trajectory of disc does NOT intercept with body)

## 4 step method

- LEFT foot forward (STRAIGHT/VERTICAL)
- RIGHT foot forward (HORIZONTAL) and LEFT foot turns 90deg to the LEFT (to be HORIZONTAL) simultaneously
- Criss cross LEFT foot forward and behind right foot (HORIZONTAL)
  - Shoulders + Waist are turning/rotating away from where you are throwing to get extra reach (i.e. winding back up, reach all the way back)
- RIGHT foot lunge out (HORIZONTAL)
  - Start reaching frisbee back simultaneously
  - When right foot plants/lands, then you should be already in motion to release the frisbee

## 5 step method

- RIGHT foot forward (STRAIGHT/VERTICAL)
- LEFT foot forward (STRAIGHT/VERTICAL)
- RIGHT foot forward (HORIZONTAL) and LEFT foot turns 90deg to the LEFT (to be HORIZONTAL) simultaneously
- Criss cross LEFT foot forward and behind right foot (HORIZONTAL)
  - Shoulders + Waist are turning/rotating away from where you are throwing to get extra reach (i.e. winding back up, reach all the way back)
- RIGHT foot lunge out (HORIZONTAL)
  - Start reaching frisbee back simultaneously
  - When right foot plants/lands, then you should be already in motion to release the frisbee

# Throwing Long Backhand

```
 _____
|     /
|    /
|   /
|  /
| /
|/
-------
```

- Disk should be very highly angled towards the ground (deg >= 45 && deg <= 90)
  - Note: Disk will flatten itself out in the air
- Planted pivot foot should be like a ballerina's foot (heel NOT touching ground) to allow for extra hip/contortional rotation
- Bring thumb closer to outer edge of disc
- All fingers (except thumb) INSIDE the disk
- Similar to pulling a door open
- Arm should bent a little at elbow
- One solid continuous pendulum motion arc (similar to golf swing, following a diagonal line)
- Wrist snap at the end (just before/after leaving the waist)
- Finish with arm/hand high in the air
